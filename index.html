AmazIf Race 2026

You will race by navigating your way around parts of Helsinki, completing tasks while you go!

NOTE: Role-specific instructions are at the bottom

RULES

There are two types of tasks - MYSTERIES and TRIVIA, and you will solve them together as a team:

  • the FINN can freely contribute to solving MYSTERIES
  • the FINN can freely help with navigation

BUT

  • the FINN cannot help in answering TRIVIA

Sometimes TRIVIA can be challenging since it may need some local knowledge or language skills, so twice during the race (at any point) you can use an ASK A LOCAL card.

ASK A LOCAL works like this:

  • You politely stop a stranger and ask if they are a local (for example, if they live in Finland).
  • You can tell them that you are a Nordic team from If Insurance racing the city, and part of the race is to ask help from a local.
  • You can ask the local any one question regarding trivia. One question per card.
  • The local can only answer YES or NO, nothing else.

General rules

  • Googling, using search engines, browsing the web or prompting AI is not allowed!
  • However, you can freely search for locations in a map application and use map directions.
